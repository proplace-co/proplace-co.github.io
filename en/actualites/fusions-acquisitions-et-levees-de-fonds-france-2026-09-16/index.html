  • ARGAN

    WDP et ARGAN fusionnent pour créer un champion européen logistique

    M&AFrance13 Md€Exited (Acquired / Buyout)

    WDP and Argan: When two real estate companies merge, Europe's logistics map is redrawn

    Source: presseagence.fr → · Sector Construction & PropTech — 📬 subscribe to the Construction & PropTech newsletter

    WDP, a Belgian real estate company specializing in logistics real estate, and Argan, its listed French counterpart, announce their merger to form an entity valued at €13 billion — the future European heavyweight in the sector.

    The immediate interpretation: a sensible consolidation between two geographically complementary players, pooling their balance sheets to gain more weight against Anglo-Saxon giants.

    What this operation truly signifies is that the logistics warehouse has become the critical infrastructure of modern commerce — as strategic as the railway network was in the 19th century. Whoever controls the distribution nodes controls the flow of goods, and thus the margins of the entire downstream value chain. The WDP-Argan merger is not a real estate transaction: it is the establishment of a toll on the physical movement of goods in Northwestern Europe. For a French investor seeking exposure to logistics real estate, the merged entity will offer unparalleled liquidity and geographical diversification in continental Europe — to be watched closely when markets open.

    M&AFranceExited (Acquired / Buyout)

    Septeo acquires Backyou: Hotel software completes its coverage

    Source: lagazettefrance.fr →

    Septeo, a European group of business software, acquires Backyou, a Saône-et-Loire-based company specializing in group, seminar, and event management for the hotel industry, present in 32 countries and supporting over 2,000 establishments. The amount was not disclosed.

The logic is that of a publisher transforming its modular offering into a complete suite: each acquisition reduces the risk that the client will look elsewhere for a complementary tool, and each added module increases the exit cost of the whole. With 17% of Backyou's revenue already generated internationally and growth exceeding 100% since January, the asset also brings commercial momentum that Septeo will be able to amplify through its 10,000 European clients.

    GSK acquires cell therapy asset from Chimagen Biosciences

    Source: gsk.com →
