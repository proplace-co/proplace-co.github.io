  • AB Dolomitas

    SigmaRoc rachète le producteur de dolomite AB Dolomitas (Lituanie) pour 118 M€

    M&ALithuania110 M€Exited (Acquired / Buyout)

    SigmaRoc acquires AB Dolomitas: **€110M** to anchor the Baltic platform

    Source: thedealwire.com →

    SigmaRoc, a London-listed group specializing in lime and industrial minerals, acquires 100% of AB Dolomitas, a Lithuanian dolomite producer founded in 1964, for €118 million in enterprise value (€110M for the core business, €8M for non-strategic assets). The structure: €90M in cash and €20M in new SigmaRoc shares.

    AB Dolomitas produces approximately 3.5 million tons per year, offers more than 40 product variants, and has estimated reserves for 25 years. Integration into SigmaRoc's existing Baltic platform is the key point: it is not about entering a new geography, but about densifying an already established presence. In industrial minerals, geographical proximity to customers (construction, chemicals, agriculture) is as effective a barrier to entry as technology — transport represents a significant portion of the final cost. Acquiring reserves for 25 years in a basin where one is already an operator is to lock in a sustainable cost advantage.

    M&ABelgiumExited (Acquired / Buyout)

    Vesta Software Group acquires Aucxis BV: auction market software and RFID

    Source: vestasoftwaregroup.com → · Sector B2B Software & Cloud — 📬 subscribe to the B2B Software & Cloud newsletter

    Vesta Software Group (Wallingford, Oxfordshire) acquires Aucxis BV (Belgium, founded in 1983), a specialist in auction platforms for fish, flower, and fresh produce markets, and industrial RFID solutions. Amount undisclosed. Vesta's first acquisition in Belgium.

Vesta, whose model is to preserve the autonomy of acquired entities while providing them with financial backing, is the type of acquirer that maximizes the value of these assets without distorting them.

    Nyrio rachète l'activité robotique médicale de BizLink (Taïwan)

    M&ATaiwanExited (Acquired / Buyout)

    Nyrio acquires BizLink's medical robotics business: from educational arm to operating room

    Source: lagazettefrance.fr →

    Nyrio (Niryo), a French manufacturer of robotic arms founded by Marc-Henri Frouin, acquires the medical robotics business of the Taiwanese group BizLink. Amount undisclosed. The transaction, finalized this summer, integrates Orion System (patient positioning) and Pulsar (modular medical robotic platform) technologies, as well as associated teams and intellectual property.

    Niryo first established itself in robotics education and training, then in light industry. The leap to medical is structurally different: regulatory certifications (CE medical, FDA) take years and are expensive — acquiring an already approved and internationally deployed business means buying regulatory time as much as technology. The global medical robotics market, estimated at $15 billion with annual growth close to 15%, is less sensitive to economic cycles than industry and less contested by Asian giants than education. For a French industrial SME seeking to move upmarket, this type of acquisition — proven technology, established clinical experience, continuity of production in France — is one of the rare ways to enter a market with high barriers without starting from scratch.

    Navan acquires BoomPop: business travel swallows professional events

    Source: infostourisme.com →

    Qualitas Energy has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Cero Generation's European renewable energy platform from Macquarie Group, representing a portfolio of 5.8 GW of solar photovoltaic and battery storage projects in the UK, Italy, and Spain. In renewables, permits, grid connections, and teams that know how to navigate local regulations are the real barrier to entry. Acquiring Cero means acquiring five years of regulatory work that cannot be recreated by buying panels.

    Hygie31 acquires Navi Group: 600 Irish pharmacies and the door to English-speaking markets

    Source: ladepeche.fr →

    Hygie31 (Lafayette pharmacies, Toulouse) finalizes the majority acquisition of Navi Group, the Irish leader in independent pharmacies, bringing together 600 pharmacies under the CarePlus and StayWell brands, for a business volume of €700 million. At €6 billion in aggregated volume, it begins to weigh in conversations that isolated pharmacies cannot have. For laboratories and wholesalers, an interlocutor of this size changes the very structure of price negotiations.

    Source: thebeautyanalyst.fr → · Sector D2C & Consumer Brands — 📬 subscribe to the D2C & Consumer Brands newsletter

    Caudalie acquires 100% of Talm, a French sensitive skin care brand founded in 2021, in which it had already been a shareholder since 2022. Amount undisclosed. Kenza Keller remains CEO with preserved operational and creative autonomy.

    Francisco Partners and KKR enter TeamSystem at **€10Bn**: secondary liquidity as a sign of maturity

    Source: pulse2.com →

    Francisco Partners and KKR acquire minority stakes in TeamSystem, an Italian provider of management software (accounting, payroll, ERP) for SMEs and liberal professions, from majority shareholder Hellman & Friedman. For French B2B software executives considering their valuation, TeamSystem is a benchmark: the depth of integration into regulatory processes is worth more, in the long run, than the theoretical addressable market size. The Cegid operation, in the background of this day, follows the same logic.

    ArcelorMittal acquiert 28,4% de Vallourec pour 955 M€

    M&AFrance955 M€Exited (Acquired / Buyout)

    ArcelorMittal acquires stake in Vallourec: €955M to secure tubes for the post-oil world

    Source: akm.ru →

    ArcelorMittal finalizes the acquisition of a 28.4% stake in Vallourec for €955 million, purchased from Apollo Global Management. The price was set in March 2024 at €14.64 per share; the transaction has just obtained all regulatory approvals. ArcelorMittal explicitly rules out any offer for the remaining shares in the next six months.

    The immediate interpretation: a global steelmaker gains a foothold in seamless tubes to diversify its outlets beyond automotive and construction.

In other words, this is not a bet on oil — it is a bet that high-precision tubes will remain indispensable in the energy transition (geothermal, CO₂ capture, hydrogen), and that a decarbonized asset is worth, regulatory and commercially, more each year. For a French investor or industrialist exposed to steelmaking, the signal is clear: value is shifting towards segments where the carbon footprint is already low, not just towards those that promise to lower it.

  • Verne

    Pony.ai et Verne lancent les premiers robotaxis autonomes en Europe à Zagreb

    M&ACroatia

    Pony.ai and Verne launch Europe's first driverless robotaxi service in Zagreb

    Source: pivotnews.ai →

    Pony.ai and Verne announce the launch of fully driverless rides on public roads in Zagreb — a 22 km journey between Verne's headquarters, the business center, and the airport, bookable via the Uber app. Pony.ai's 7th generation system drives the vehicle (a modified Chinese Arcfox Alpha T5); Verne provides remote supervision.

    The real obstacle is not technological — it is regulatory and geographical. European approval for a fully automated vehicle goes through the "small series" procedure (Article 41 of the type-approval regulation), capped at 1,500 units per year according to the German KBA authority. However, Pony.ai and Uber have announced more than 2,000 robotaxis on European roads in five cities. The dependence on Croatian authorization — which stops at the border — is therefore the real constraint: Zagreb is a legal laboratory as much as a commercial service. For investors in autonomous mobility, the question is not whether the technology works, but whether the European regulatory framework can evolve quickly enough to allow a viable economy of scale.

    Beelzebub lève 3 M€ en seed pour sa plateforme de cybersécurité native IA

    FundraisingItaly3 M€Seed

    Beelzebub raises **€3M** in seed: cybersecurity by deception against AI attacks

    Source: beinsure.com → · Sector Cybersecurity — 📬 subscribe to the Cybersecurity newsletter

    Beelzebub (Milan, founded in 2021 as an open-source project, incorporated in July 2025) raises €3 million in seed from United Ventures, bringing the total raised to €3.3M. The platform detects automated and AI-driven attacks by diverting attackers to decoy environments, then analyzes their behavior.

    The "assumed breach" approach — assuming the attacker is already in the network — is now the norm in critical infrastructure security. What distinguishes Beelzebub is the use of decoys based on language models (LLMs) that mimic real servers, databases, and APIs: the attacker, whether human or automated, interacts with an environment that appears authentic while being entirely controlled. This is a direct response to the rise of AI-driven attacks, which saturate classic perimeter defenses by their speed and volume. The seed round funds commercial expansion (offices in Rome and San Francisco) — the bet is that critical infrastructure operators, under increasing regulatory pressure (NIS2 in Europe), constitute a natural first market.

    BIOWEG lève 30 M€ en série A pour sa production de cellulose bactérienne

    FundraisingGermany30 M€Series A

    BIOWEG raises **€30M** in Series A: bacterial cellulose enters the industrial era

    Source: startupmafia.eu →

    BIOWEG (Elsdorf, Germany) closes a Series A round at €30 million and announces the construction of a large-scale bacterial cellulose plant in Germany.

    Bacterial cellulose is a biomaterial produced by fermentation, with remarkable mechanical properties (strength, flexibility, biocompatibility), with applications in packaging, textiles, medical, and cosmetics. The peculiarity of this fundraising is its use: to build a factory, not to finance R&D. BIOWEG is moving from the laboratory stage to the industrial stage, which is the most difficult and costly leap in green chemistry. The €30M is a down payment for an infrastructure that will probably cost much more — but it signals that investors have validated the technical and economic feasibility at scale. This is the type of gamble Europe needs to make for its green chemistry to remain competitive against American and Asian giants.

    IVEX lève 5 M€ pour tester la sécurité de l'IA automobile

    FundraisingBelgium5 M€Seed

    IVEX raises **€5M** in Series A: AI accelerates validation of automotive safety systems

    Source: aiineurope.co →

    IVEX (Belgium, KU Leuven spin-out, founded in 2018) raises €5 million in Series A to accelerate its development. The company automates the validation of advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) — automatic braking, lane keeping — by replacing manual review of sensor data with AI tools.

    The problem IVEX solves is real and growing: each new generation of ADAS generates volumes of test data that engineering teams can no longer process manually at a reasonable cost. European regulation on automotive safety (UNECE WP.29, validation requirements for automated systems) creates a structural demand for such tools — not because manufacturers want them, but because they have no choice. IVEX positions itself on a regulatory bottleneck, which is one of the best positions in industrial B2B.

CS Group (Sopra Steria) and AIKO: a memorandum of understanding for sovereign space operations

CS Group, a subsidiary of Sopra Steria, and the Italian startup AIKO have signed a memorandum of understanding aimed at combining critical systems and European-developed artificial intelligence for satellite operations and space surveillance. No amount, no equity stake: this is a Franco-Italian technological alliance.

An MoU remains a light commitment. What matters here is the positioning: operational space is becoming a field where sovereignty is explicitly the commercial argument. This is less a financial operation than a declaration of intent in a market where public tenders precisely reward this positioning.
