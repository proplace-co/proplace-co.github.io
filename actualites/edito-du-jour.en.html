Healthcare Activos acquires the real estate of two mental health clinics in Germany

Source: capital-riesgo.es →

📬 Subscribe to the HealthTech & Digital Health newsletter

Healthcare Activos is a real estate investment platform specializing in healthcare assets, present in Spain, Germany, Portugal, and France: it acquires the real estate of healthcare facilities and leases them to operators. It has just acquired two mental health clinics in Germany for over 50 million euros. According to specialized Spanish press (Alimarket), the seller, the Median healthcare group, continues to operate them under a sale-and-leaseback arrangement.

The first-level interpretation is that of a real estate company strengthening its position in a sought-after segment: clinic properties leased long-term to an established operator, in a specialty where demand for care remains strong.

What deserves attention is the mechanics of the sale-and-leaseback: the operator frees up capital without leaving its premises, and the real estate company acquires a lease backed by a healthcare activity. For a French healthcare group, this is a financing lever to examine closely; for an investor, it is a sign that specialized clinic properties are becoming a distinct asset class in Europe.

Cohere signs a letter of intent to acquire Aisle and its AI-powered vulnerability detection

Source: reuters.com →

📬 Subscribe to the Cybersecurity newsletter

Cohere is a Canadian artificial intelligence lab specializing in large language models, primarily for businesses. Aisle is a San Francisco startup that uses AI to detect vulnerabilities in software code. Cohere announced it has signed a letter of intent to acquire Aisle; the amount has not been disclosed.

The obvious interpretation: an AI lab absorbing a security component to enrich its enterprise offering, at a time when large organizations are hesitant to deploy generative models without guarantees on the integrity of their code.

The move is more precise than that. Vulnerability detection in code is one of the use cases where language models show measurable and verifiable results — a field where the added value of AI can be proven line by line, not just promised in a demo. By integrating Aisle, Cohere is not just adding a feature: it is equipping itself with a selling point for CIOs and CISOs, the two functions that most often block large-scale deployments. This is an acquisition of credibility as much as technology. For European players in application cybersecurity, the operation confirms that generative AI labs are now active acquirers in their space — not just potential partners.

Holo automates marketing for small businesses and raises funds to accelerate from Vilnius

Source: eu-startups.com →

📬 Subscribe to the Horizontal & Productivity SaaS newsletter

Holo is a Lithuanian platform founded in Vilnius that automatically plans and executes marketing campaigns for small businesses — positioning itself as a marketing "autopilot" for structures that lack the means or time to manage this internally. The company has just closed a round of 5 million euros in Seed, with the goal of accelerating product development, expanding its SME customer base, and strengthening its teams in Vilnius.

The segment is well-identified: mainstream marketing tools (Meta Ads, Google, Mailchimp) require a level of skill and availability that most very small businesses do not possess. Holo bets that AI can bridge this gap by making execution almost transparent for the business owner. The real test will be retention — automating campaigns is one thing, proving that they generate measurable returns for an artisan or a local business is another. This is where the company's ability to transition from Seed to Series A will be determined.
